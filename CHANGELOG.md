# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version.

- Opens JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and TIFF, with instant next/previous and animation playback.
- Zoom, pan, rotate, flip, fullscreen and an info sidebar with EXIF, location and colour profile.
- Non-destructive editing: crop, resize, colour adjustments, drawing, selections and clipboard. Edits are only written when you save a copy.
- EXIF editing, always on a copy of the file.
- Optional local AI tools: Remove Background, Denoise and Enhance, with optional NVIDIA GPU support.
- Delete an image to the system Trash, with a confirmation; Ctrl+Z restores it.
- Six languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese.
